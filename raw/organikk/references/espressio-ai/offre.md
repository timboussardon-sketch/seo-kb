# Espressio.ai : offre, process, pricing, FAQ

> Scrapé le 2026-06-09. Détail des systèmes packagés et du modèle commercial.

## Les systèmes packagés

L'offre n'est pas vendue en heures mais en **systèmes nommés**, chacun présenté comme une boucle fermée qui tourne en autopilote. C'est le coeur du modèle à transposer pour Organikk.

### Content OS

**Pitch :** « AI agents that research, write, and publish your content on autopilot. »

Trois modules :
1. **Research Engine** : Niche Monitoring (competitor tracking, trend detection, engagement analysis) + Research Database (top-performing formats, hook patterns, content gaps)
2. **Content Production** : Idea Generation (hook variants, key talking points, format selection) + AI Writing (voice matching, AI pattern removal) + Quality Gate (content score, voice score, pass/revise)
3. **Publish & Optimize** : Scheduling (multi-platform, optimal timing) + Profile Optimization (profile scoring, benchmarking) + Performance Loop (réinjecté dans le Research Engine)

**Boucle :** Research → Ideate → Write + QA → Publish. La data de performance réalimente le Research Engine en continu.

> Très proche du content-brain / SyntheticBrain de Tim. Le « Quality Gate » + « AI pattern removal » = exactement la quality gate anti-IA writing. Argument : Organikk a déjà ce système, en prod.

### RevenueOS

**Pitch :** « Meeting intel and proposal creation, one system. Calls get recorded, broken down, and turned into a branded proposal the same day. »

Trois étapes :
1. **Capture & Analyze** : auto-transcript, extraction (needs, budget, goals, timeline, objections), deal brief en minutes, push vers CRM/Slack
2. **Build** : l'IA écrit (scope of work, service mapping, pricing packages) + auto-branding (logo client) + deck 18+ slides assemblé
3. **Ship** : team review (preview/edit/approve) + auto-deploy (site de proposition, password-locked) + lien unique pour le client

**Boucle :** Call Ends → Deal Brief → Proposal Ships → Same Day

> Proche des espaces clients HTML noindex d'Organikk (DA Leexi). Le « proposal site password-locked » = le format espace client.

### Lead Generation

« Targeted prospects found, enriched, and followed up with personalised outreach. Built around real signals, not guesswork. »

### Competitive Intel

« Always-on monitoring of competitor moves, pricing, and market shifts. Delivered as a weekly brief. No analyst needed. »

> Note : sur la home le 3e service est « Competitive Intel », mais Lead Generation et Competitive Intel coexistent selon les sections. L'offre réelle = Content OS + RevenueOS en flagship + custom workflows à la demande.

## Process commercial (4 phases)

1. **Strategy Call** (30 min) : où l'IA s'insère
2. **Workshop** (2h avec l'équipe) : roadmap d'implémentation complète
3. **Build & Deploy** (semaines 2-3) : premier système branché sur le workflow existant
4. **Optimize & Expand** (semaine 4+) : reviews mensuelles, on reste embedded, on scale aux systèmes suivants quand le premier prouve son ROI

Délai annoncé : système live en 2 à 4 semaines.

## FAQ (4 catégories)

**Getting Started**
- What does Espressio actually do ? « We build and deploy AI systems that handle repeatable marketing work. »
- Do I need to be technical ? « Not at all. We handle the build, deployment, and optimization. »
- Who is this for ? « Growth teams, marketing leads, and founders at companies doing $1M+ in revenue. »
- How is this different from a freelancer/agency ? « Freelancers and agencies add headcount to your execution. We replace the execution itself. »

**Services & Systems**
- What is Content OS / Revenue OS ? (cf. ci-dessus)
- Can you build custom systems ? « Yes. Those are our flagship products, but we build custom AI workflows for anything repetitive. »
- Will the content sound like AI ? « No. Every system includes voice matching and an AI pattern removal layer. »

**Process & Timeline**
- How fast ? « Most systems are live within 2 to 4 weeks. »
- What if I just want the roadmap ? « We offer a standalone strategy session. »
- How involved does my team need to be ? « Light involvement. 2 to 3 hours in the first week. »

**Pricing & Support**
- How much ? « Pricing depends on scope. We give you a clear proposal with fixed pricing. »
- Long-term commitment ? « No. Monthly cycles. Pause or stop anytime. »
- After the system is live ? « We stay embedded until it works. Once stable, we step back. You own everything we build. »
- Examples of your work ? « Reach out and we'll share relevant case studies. »

## Pricing

Aucun prix affiché. Fixed pricing donné dans la proposition après scoping. Outcome-based pricing revendiqué, cycles mensuels sans engagement.
